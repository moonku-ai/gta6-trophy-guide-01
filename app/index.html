Roadmap to the Platinum — 5 Stages, About 200 Hours

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1 to 5 (60–80 hours). Chapter trophies and mission trophies pop as you go and cannot be missed. Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 before the chapter ends. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.
  2. Stage 2 — Collectibles and exploration. WAiNK It at 100 locations, 3 Collectible Card sets, 100 vehicles discovered, and explore all of Leonida. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go: 100% completion needs 200 of 277 base-model vehicles and 100 of 255 animals.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. 250 stunts, all 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 20 races won, Top Fun Drone Races at all 14 locations, 50 challenges (70 of 80 count toward 100%).
  4. Stage 4 — Cleanup. The five character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the robberies (5 bank vaults, armored truck, jewelry store, one illegal operation per gang) and the money trophies ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all 8 garages).
  5. Stage 5 — Platinum. Finish the 100% checklist for A Long Road, then Vice Got a Price! pops with the last trophy.

Missable — 1 possible: Positive Mental Attitude. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation disappears after Chapter 1, and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it, and keep a Chapter 1 save. Everything else can be finished after the story in free roam.

Cheats disable trophies and saving, per GTABase and TechWiser — keep cheats off for the whole run.
