A complete, hands-on trophy companion for Grand Theft Auto VI. It gathers every trophy in one place and walks you through each one step by step, from the story trophies you pick up naturally to the trickier collectibles and challenges that are easy to miss on a first playthrough.

Every trophy entry comes with a clear explanation of what to do, where to go, and what to watch out for, backed by cited sources so you can check the details yourself. A built-in tracker lets you tick off trophies as you earn them, and a roadmap lays out a sensible order of play so you can aim for the platinum without backtracking through the whole game. A spoiler mode keeps story details hidden until you choose to reveal them, so the guide stays useful whether you are still early in the story or mopping up the last few trophies.

## Run it

```bash
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
